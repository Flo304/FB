Research and development of new materials from recycled synthetic textiles : Fablab Brussles x PetitRien
This project was made in collaboration with Yaritza Laverde, Julie Pacher, Louise Limontas and Camille Vierin

Recipe

- Alginate is the binder
- Water
- Calcium Chloride for waterproofing
- Organic or synthetic textile

Using alginate and natural fiber textile has a better result due to the chemical reaction between the two materials. All examples in this research is synthetic textile based.
